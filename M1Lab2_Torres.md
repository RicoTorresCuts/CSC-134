# The Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The bread, the peanut butter jar, and a knife are all present.
- bread is sliced inside bag

## Steps
- remove twist tie
- open the bread bag 
- reach into bag, grab 2 pieces of bread
- put bread slices side by side
- grab jar, move near bread
- unscrew the jar lid
- break seal to get to the peanut butter
- put knife in the jar
- get peanut butter on the knife
- put peanut butter on the bread
- place one slice into another
- put sandwich on a plate
- put knife in kitchen sink
- close peanut butter jar
- put loaf back in the bag
- twist bag clockwise
- put twist tie back on the bag

## Done when
- plate is empty
